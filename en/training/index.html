TRAINING / 03

The process

Five steps from problem to habit.

  1. 01

    Initial consultation

    We clarify environment, history, daily routine and the behaviour you want to change.

  2. 02

    Tailored plan

    We set realistic goals, motivation, rules and exercises for the specific team.

  3. 03

    Clear foundations

    We build understandable signals, handler engagement and core commands without unnecessary noise.

  4. 04

    Real distractions

    We transfer the skills around movement, people, dogs, urban noise and changing distances.

  5. 05

    A habit for both

    The dog reinforces the behaviour while the handler learns how to maintain the result.

Before the first session

Short, honest answers.

Is it suitable for every breed?+

The plan begins with temperament, age, health and environment — not a breed label. Specific cases start with an assessment.

How many sessions are needed?+

There is no honest universal number. The initial consultation helps define the direction and a suitable pace.

Does the handler work too?+

Yes. Lasting change requires the person to understand signals, timing and rules just as well as the dog.

Can a child participate?+

Handlers under 18 participate with a parent and under trainer supervision.
